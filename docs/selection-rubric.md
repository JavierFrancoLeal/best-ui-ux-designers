# Antifragile Selection Rubric — 4 Binary Filters & Via Negativa Blacklist

To insulate `best-ui-ux-designers` from celebrity inflation, self-referential industry awards, and transient stylistic trends, admission to the 12-designer Barbell roster follows the **Wittgenstein / Lindy Rule**: jury awards (`Awwwards`, `Dribbble`), follower counts, and conference keynotes are strictly inadmissible as evidence.

Every candidate MUST pass **4 of 4 mandatory binary admission filters** and remain completely free of all five **Via Negativa Blacklist** disqualifiers.

---

## 1. Mandatory Binary Admission Filters (4 of 4 Required)

Failing any single filter triggers an automatic `FAIL` with zero compensatory weighting.

| Filter | Falsifiable Operational Definition | Binary PASS Threshold | Rejected Evidence (FAIL) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F1. Verifiable Lindy Survival (or Physical/Cognitive Invariant Anchor)** | Principles must have survived at least two full computing platform shifts (`>= 20` years for the Lindy Pole) or, on the Convex Frontier, anchor directly in measurable perception, latency, or software engineering invariants. | **Lindy Pole:** Formulated `>= 20` years ago and empirically operative in 2026. **Convex Pole:** Primitives or architectures proven across `>= 3` years of real production operation. | Seasonal aesthetics (`< 24` months), trendy palettes, or speculative manifestos without temporal survival. |
| **F2. Measurable Voluntary Adoption** | End users or engineers adopt the artifact voluntarily—often paying or migrating away from pre-installed incumbents—without corporate procurement mandates or regulatory captivity. | At least one shipped product, library, or standard with verifiable mass voluntary adoption (`> 1M` active users, `> 10,000` paying teams, OSS libraries with millions of weekly downloads, or multi-decade continuous hardware production). | IT-mandated enterprise software with zero user choice, unpublished portfolio concepts, or showcase pieces without daily use. |
| **F3. Forced Competitor Imitation** | Incumbent competitors are compelled to replicate the candidate's interaction pattern to stem user or developer churn, demonstrating structural competitive asymmetry. | Verifiable historical evidence that `>= 2` category leaders replicated the pattern (e.g., Apple adopting Braun/Rams canon; mainstream browsers adopting vertical tabs; SaaS adopting `Cmd+K` and local-first sync). | Rhetorical praise in blogs or guild awards without structural replication in rival products. |
| **F4. Resilience Under Dirty Data, Latency & Fatigue** | The methodology holds under hostile non-ideal conditions: extreme strings, massive lists, slow networks, partial failures, accessibility constraints, and fatigued operators. | Explicit, documented rules for empty/error/loading states, error prevention under distraction, graceful degradation, or legibility under constrained hardware. | Designs validated only in static `400x300 px` shots, synthetic clean data, or happy-path demos lacking error paths. |
